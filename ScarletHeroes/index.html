About these simulations

Independent learning aid based on Kevin Crawford’s Scarlet Heroes, “Playing the Game,” printed pp. 16–23. Scenes, heroes and dialogue are original teaching examples.

Both roles are scripted locally, with deliberately chosen rolls so every lesson demonstrates its rule. This is not a live AI GM or a random campaign. Restart repeats the same example. Selected aspects of the chapter are covered, not every rule.

Page references use the printed book numbers. Consult your book for full rules and GM judgment.
