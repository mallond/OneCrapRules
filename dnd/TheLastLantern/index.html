The expedition journal

    You roll the dice. The dungeon keeps the score. No virtual rolls.

    The rules, before you descend

    Your objective

    Resolve seven chambers. Chambers 2, 4 and 6 each hold a seal, recovered when the encounter ends. Defeat the Keeper in chamber 7 with all three seals to win. At 0 vitality or 0 lantern fuel, your expedition ends.

    Start with 20 vitality, 10 fuel and one potion. Each descent uses 1 fuel. Between chambers, a potion heals a d12 (maximum 20); it is consumed when you choose it. Play is held in this page; refreshing starts a new expedition.

    Explore and choose

    Roll d6: 1–2 creature, 3 trap, 4 cache, 5 spring, 6 lantern shrine. Creatures and traps let you choose: bold earns 5 extra gold if you survive; careful spends 1 fuel for +3 on your d20 checks for that encounter. Careful requires at least 2 fuel.

    Your dice, your fate

    • d20: meet the shown target to hit or evade. Natural 1 always fails; natural 20 always succeeds and doubles attack damage.
    • d8: your damage on a hit. Creatures only retaliate after a missed attack.
    • d4: damage you suffer after a failed check.
    • d10: treasure, plus twice the chamber number.
    • d12: healing from springs or your potion.
    • d6: chamber type, or fuel restored at a shrine (maximum 10).
    • d100: the final relic. Roll percentile tens and units; 00 + 0 means 100. Enter the combined number 1–100.

    Your score is gold + 25 per seal + remaining vitality + 5 per remaining fuel. This is an original game, not official D&D rules.
